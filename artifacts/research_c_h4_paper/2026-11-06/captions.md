## Figure 1. Existing method and resource dependencies

Conceptual workflow of PR-aware DF: signed symmetric coefficients J and frame-specific orthogonal rotations U define a number-CDF representation; a smooth mapped nonidentity tail-mass penalty accompanies tensor fitting. The frame order and head/tail mask remain fixed. Candidate-specific PF/RTE references feed conditional RPE shot allocation, while P0-compiled kernels and separate head/X/Y shells feed native pricing. Conditional total RZ is the product of repetition weight W and mean kernel RZ plus shell cost. U affects tensor fitting, noncommuting references and native rotations, even though the fixed-J tail-mass penalty does not depend directly on U. The original-g correction and scalar accounting are preserved. This diagram depicts existing definitions and dependencies, not a new algorithm or an unperformed numerical experiment. Sources: `c_pr_aware_df.py`, `c_near_objective.py`, `c_near_r32_statistics.py` at the science-results commit; [prior mathematical/literature comparison](../../../docs/research/research_c_related_work_novelty_evidence_20261105.md), originally fixed at 1a588f5491bce394ecf039ee4d2de57581e2e0b1. This schematic has no estimated interval or statistical claim.
