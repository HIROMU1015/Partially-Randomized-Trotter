# Methods — saved-input resource comparison

## Scope and evidence units

We report fixed finite-sample resource comparisons for linear H4, H6, and H8 chains with adjacent separation 1.0 Å in STO-3G, at T = 0.8 and P = 0. The main table contains six fixed independent-confirmation comparisons: two endpoints per system. Their candidate IDs, DF policies/ranks, L_D, q/r/K, δ windows, sample sizes, PF controls, exact saved costs, and paired standard errors are specified in Table 1 and its unchanged source records. The table is distinct from the legacy/exploratory mechanism comparisons in Figure 2A. Figure 2B reuses the same two H8 independent n48 observations as the main table. It supplies a mechanism view of those observations and does not add a confirmation cohort.

The molecular Hamiltonian, state, snapshot, compiler settings, candidate/control identities, and cohort must be joined through their fixed provenance, not by candidate name or DF rank alone. H4 legacy fixed DF12 and the later H4 tol-only DF10 series remain separate. New exploratory n8, saved FP n32, selected old confirmation-union n32, and independent confirmation samples are not pooled. H2 is outside this manuscript integration.

## Electronic structure and DF input preparation

The public H4 input SOURCE uses the OpenFermion-PySCF helpers to obtain RHF canonical orbitals and spatial integrals, maps them to alternating alpha/beta spin orbitals, and applies the saved one-half convention for the two-body tensor. The saved policy comparison reports RHF convergence tolerance 1e-9, maximum 50 cycles, Angstrom coordinates, no spatial symmetry, and canonical helper order without an additional orbital sort for the compared H4/H6/H8 inputs. This is a recorded agreement of settings, not a fresh independent verification of every upstream provider, orbital phase/order, library version, or original integral byte.

For the main-table series the recorded DF policy is OpenFermion low_rank_two_body_decomposition with truncation threshold 1e-8 and no supplied final_rank. Returned generation order and signed coefficients are retained, with no subsequent coefficient cutoff. The resulting saved ranks are H4 DF10, H6 DF19, and H8 DF28. A weighted Hermitian-projection engineering check is recorded, with the system-dependent full-Fock mode count; its shared definition does not imply identical finite-precision inputs or an interval certificate. The H4 legacy DF12 series in Figure 2A follows its earlier fixed-rank input policy and is not relabelled tol-only.

The scientific input snapshots are reused as saved. No SCF, integral, DF, state, or reference calculation was performed for this document preparation. Identifiers and settings do not prove that all preparation rules are identical across systems.

## State solver, phase, normalization, and residual exceptions

The H4 tol-only input SOURCE constructs the full matrix in the Nα = Nβ = 2 physical sector (dimension 36), calls scipy.linalg.eigh, selects the lowest eigenvector, and fixes its canonical phase. It uses the normalized eigenvector returned by eigh and checks its saved residual against 1e-10. The SOURCE records no renormalization after saving; the later loader checks the saved norm and identity rather than silently changing the vector.

The public policy-comparison record describes H6 and H8 as using the lowest state in the Nα = Nβ = 3/4 sectors (dimensions 400/4900), obtained with eigsh, which = SA, a Hartree-Fock initial vector, k = 1, tolerance 1e-12, ncv = 40, and maximum 1000 iterations. That record specifies one binary64 normalization, the canonical phase convention, and absolute 1e-9 plus relative 1e-10 residual checks. These H6/H8 settings and gates are transcribed from the saved comparison record; their complete original provider/input witnesses were not re-audited here. The available generic solver SOURCE contains an explicit normalization step, but its default arguments are not evidence that a specific historical run used those defaults.

Thus the H4 dense eigensolver path differs from the recorded H6/H8 iterative path, including normalization and residual gates. We retain the original vectors and their bindings. A small recorded residual is not a ground-state certification or a common degeneracy-handling proof. We do not infer complete procedural identity or attribute between-system differences solely to molecular size. The exception table documents the source level and remaining uncertainty for each statement.

## Circuits, compiler, and the cost observable

The saved comparisons use full cosine and sine Hadamard wrappers, symmetric_directional control, the original scalar constant plus extracted tail identity exactly once, and an unrotated saved reference. The ordinary_ancilla_branch check in the H8 SOURCE describes low-branch identity/high-branch evolution semantics; it is not evidence of a switch to a different lowering policy. The recorded compiler options are Qiskit 1.3.0 with rz/sx/x/cx, optimization level 1, transpiler seed 17, and no target/backend topology or coupling constraints. Settings equality and matching identifiers do not establish equality of all historical compiler outputs. The original H8 profile bytes and full historical output identity remain unverified from public evidence.

For each trajectory, the cosine and sine compiled RZ counts are paired with the saved analytic shot requirements. The reported mean and paired SE are copied from completed results, without recomputing the trajectory costs or their variance. The SE describes sampling of trajectory cost, not physical quantum measurement shots. No new ratios, cost differences, confidence intervals, p-values, power calculations, or mechanism decompositions were computed here. Exact cost tokens are retained without new decimal conversion or rounding.

The six fixed PF controls in Table 1 use direct measured compiled-axis prices. This does not make the full registered deterministic domains directly measured: the legacy/exploratory evidence includes model estimates, explicitly marked in Figure 2A and the existing manifests. Compiled RZ is the named gate metric, not an angle-classified non-Clifford count, a synthesized T count, or a full state-preparation/energy/RPE cost.

## Independent confirmation and interpretation of direction

The fixed independent sample sizes are H4 n1108/n78, H6 n48/n104, and H8 n48/n48 for ε = 0.05/0.0005, respectively. The completed source results are used without candidate reselection or pooling. H4 at ε = 0.05 is NOT_REPRODUCED, with lower PF point cost; H4 at ε = 0.0005 is REPRODUCED, with lower PR point cost. Both H6 direction tests are REPRODUCED, but the coarse endpoint reproduces the pre-registered PF-favouring direction, whereas the fine endpoint favours PR. H8 reports POINT_REPRODUCED and lower PR point cost at both endpoints. Non-reproduction is an accepted outcome, not a technical failure requiring another run.

The H4 sample-size design is a finite-point planning calculation, not a certification of realized power or population-mean ordering. Different seed values do not by themselves prove statistical independence. H8 reused completed axes within the same fixed confirmation cohort; the public report and projection distinguish that reuse from pooling exploratory or old confirmation samples. A design marked unapproved at its creation is evidence of the historical design only, not proof of a later execution grant.

## Rare events and limits of inference

Rare-event observations are quoted from the saved summaries or public report, with their provenance in the existing evidence ledger. The H4 summaries include observed S2 events; the H6 summaries include observed order-2 events; the H8 report states that its confirmation observed only order-0 events. At K = 2, order 4 is outside the truncated support; at K = 4, an unobserved order-4 event remains a potentially relevant unobserved contribution. Unobserved multi-event strata and their costs or variances are not set to zero. Observed zeros or a small saved SE do not bound unseen contributions. Finite-sample point ranks, empirical signed-bias allowances, and saved SEs are not population-mean guarantees, selection-adjusted coverage, or rigorous bias certificates.

## H8 public evidence and completion limits

The public H8 confirmation result CSV and paired-axis projection, its confirmation and parallel SOURCE, its historical design, the selected-PF six-axis receipt, and existing public audit reports were read. The selected-PF receipt contains measured axes, compiler identifiers, fingerprints, and recorded exit/reap information for those deterministic jobs. Those records do not verify the later PR confirmation workers. The PR result report states successful local completion, but the detailed PR grant/seed/runtime/provider/ledger/native-exit/final-byte witnesses are not included in the public result projection. Their absence from the checked public evidence is not a claim that they do not exist privately.

Accordingly, public SOURCE policies and saved result projections are verified at the published-file level; actual PR execution completion remains reported rather than publicly re-verified through full receipts. Original H8 profile bytes and compiler-output identity remain unresolved. No private witness was opened or republished for this task, no raw event replay was undertaken, and no new scientific calculation was used to fill these gaps. The H8 evidence-status table makes these distinctions explicit.
