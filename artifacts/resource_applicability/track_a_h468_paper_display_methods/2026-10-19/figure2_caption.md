# Figure 2 — manuscript caption

**Figure 2 | Resource-cost mechanisms in the saved legacy/exploratory series and the H8 independent confirmation.** All panels refer to linear hydrogen chains with adjacent separation 1.0 Å in STO-3G, the saved DF Hamiltonians and states, evolution time T = 0.8, P = 0, and the usual signed-bias accounting with allowance factor 1. Costs combine saved analytic shot requirements with compiled RZ counts for full cosine/sine Hadamard wrappers (Qiskit 1.3.0; rz, sx, x, cx; optimization level 1; transpiler seed 17; no topology).

**(A)** Saved axis-cost ratios R_C,a, axis-shot ratios R_N,a, and total-cost ratios R_G for the legacy/exploratory comparisons at ε = 0.05, 0.005, and 0.0005. Columns show H4 **legacy fixed DF12**, H6 tol-only DF19, and H8 tol-only DF28. Blue markers denote the old n32 series; orange markers denote the new exploratory n8 series. The H4 old n32 values are selection/estimation anchors, whereas the H6/H8 old n32 values belong to selected confirmation unions. Cosine and sine axes use circles and squares. Filled PF markers denote direct measurements, and open markers denote model estimates; the intermediate-precision H6 PF remains model based. The ε categories are not interpolated. Candidate IDs, L_D, q, r, K, and the saved δ windows are specified in the unchanged display_plan.csv and core_axes.csv listed in the display manifest. This panel contains neither the new H4 tol-only DF10 independent samples nor the new H6 independent confirmation samples in Table 1.

**(B)** Saved mechanism decomposition for the two fixed H8 tol-only DF28 independent confirmation comparisons, each with n = 48: ε = 0.05, B2_p8_q2_2nd_r4_K4 (L_D = 8, δ = 0.4) versus B0I_p11_q1_2nd; and ε = 0.0005, B2_p7_q16_2nd_r4_K4 (L_D = 7, δ = 0.05) versus B0I_p15_q4_4th. The upper panel shows saved R_N, R_Ceff, and R_G; the lower panel separates nominal normalization, empirical margin, headroom, alpha-log, and rounding contributions. Nominal normalization and empirical log margin are distinct factors. C_eff depends on each method's shot allocation and is not a causal measure of compiler contribution. These are the same H8 confirmation observations shown in Table 1 and in the existing Mechanism32 records H8_CONFIRM_1/3, not additional independent evidence.
