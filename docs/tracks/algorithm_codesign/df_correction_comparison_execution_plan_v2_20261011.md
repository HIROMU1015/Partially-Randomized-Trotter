## Native semantic guards

Ideal frames, generator assembly and the source spectral reference are separate error terms.
Compiled A is compared with an independent spectral reference, retaining strict joint controlled
phase. Prior fingerprints/settings hashes must match; no compiler fallback on mismatch.
The native Operator forward-error model is conditional IEEE/BLAS plus faithful libm4ulp,
not a residual-only certificate. All assumptions and bounds are saved.

B lowering implements H/S basis changes in native rz/sx/x with their symbolic global phases,
parity compute, signed RZ or controlled signed RZ, and exact reverse basis/parity.
The local control-zero block is identity. No postselection. All Pauli labels have their own
analytic basis costs; first support-weight representative compiles validate that lowering.
X/Y/Z composition is not replaced by a representative's 1Q count.
Clifford-angle approximation distances, atan-vs-binary64 distance and global W phase distance
enter the numerical allowance. Unsupported phase or incomplete keys never produces a full T claim.

`terminal_status.json` is distinct from individual success/prefix files. Process failure, guard
failure or incomplete stage gives TECHNICAL_INCONCLUSIVE, irrespective of prefix values.

contract/marker identities, retries0 and mandatory STOP. No scientific winner field is populated.
