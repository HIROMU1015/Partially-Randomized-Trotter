## A. Auxiliary application example: an H4 DF model

We connected the existing return-aggregation generators to a reconstructed double-factorized model of linear H4 at a separation of 1.00 Å in STO-3G, with DF rank 12, a generation-prefix split L_D=3, eight system qubits, thirteen basis definitions, and 324 signed labels. The reconstructed binary64 coefficients were adopted as exact dyadic inputs. This R1 input has distinct provenance from the historical PR-2 coefficients; numerical agreement with the saved DF operator does not establish a rigorous model-binding certificate. [R1 input](https://github.com/HIROMU1015/Partially-Randomized-Trotter/blob/2937e6b655ba3cd77f982d6901d766355a8b92c1/docs/research/track_b_c1_1_r1_new_partition_exact_model_20261021/handoff.md), [binding status](https://github.com/HIROMU1015/Partially-Randomized-Trotter/blob/2937e6b655ba3cd77f982d6901d766355a8b92c1/docs/research/track_b_c1_1_r1_new_partition_exact_model_20261021/result/model_binding.json).

The adapter retained label signs, circuit-time word order, actual adjoints, and controlled relative phases. Saved local matrices for all thirteen bases (412 local operations) were recovered bitwise. Separate numerical correctness checks passed for six preselected events, including full controlled operators and X/Y diagnostics. These checks establish a limited implementation result rather than uniform certification over all labels, words, or native realizations. [fixed correctness evidence](https://github.com/HIROMU1015/Partially-Randomized-Trotter/blob/068f0ae8771fccf28c529bda12a84abe8b7e7648/docs/research/track_b_c1_3_r2_reference_recovery_c1_4_20261021/CORRECTNESS_REPORT.md).

For T=4/5, q_outer=1, four tail microsteps of duration 1/5, and degree m=5, we obtained exploratory records for 1024 whole paths per method: Ordinary RTE, General Full Return, and Closed P5. The diagnostic circuit scope was TAIL_ONLY_PLUS_SCALAR. No valid zero path was observed. The saved mean logical instruction counts per classical path were 285.125, 284.6494140625, and 284.7666015625, respectively; corresponding depths were 76.673828125, 76.619140625, and 76.6455078125. Full and Closed P5 each had 4073 degree-zero parent microsteps out of 4096. These counts describe this limited sample and do not establish a statistically resolved or native resource advantage. [saved three-method table](https://github.com/HIROMU1015/Partially-Randomized-Trotter/blob/bb9059bacb697e1105e63c1b3826b0c34886ad15/docs/research/track_b_c1_5a_p5_worker_recovery_20261021/comparison.md), [literal summary values](quoted_resource_values.json).

Classical processing was substantial in the measured implementations: the Full sampling/checking/event-evidence stage used 4410.007131644 CPU seconds, while Closed P5 required 283.993160344 CPU seconds for its normalizer/completion setup. These are implementation-specific observations, with different concurrency, cache, selector, monitoring, and timing boundaries across the two executions. They do not define an optimized or universal CPU ordering. [cost boundaries](https://github.com/HIROMU1015/Partially-Randomized-Trotter/blob/bb9059bacb697e1105e63c1b3826b0c34886ad15/docs/research/track_b_c1_5a_p5_worker_recovery_20261021/RESOURCE_REPORT.md).

## B. Limitations paragraph

In this short-time application, most sampled parents had Taylor degree zero, and the measured logical differences were small. Degree-zero parents still require child rotations; they are not zero-cost identity circuits. A setting with few nonempty return parents may therefore offer limited scope for savings in this measured circuit layer. This observation is restricted to the present input and parameters and is not evidence against the mathematical return-aggregation construction in general. [observed degree/circuit structure](https://github.com/HIROMU1015/Partially-Randomized-Trotter/blob/bb9059bacb697e1105e63c1b3826b0c34886ad15/docs/research/track_b_c1_5a_p5_worker_recovery_20261021/comparison.md).

For degree five, General Full Return and Closed P5 share the same ideal acceptance-conditioned event ensemble under the existing construction. Full retains pre-quantum zero/rejection trials, and finite-bit proposals, rational coefficient approximations, weights, certification conditions, and generation costs must be accounted for separately. Equality of an ideal ensemble does not imply identical digital trajectories or total costs. [Existing method note §§3–5,9](https://github.com/HIROMU1015/Partially-Randomized-Trotter/blob/243eea8e840f3c472753f7935e510391626fd325/docs/manuscripts/track_b_return_aggregation_native_resource_note_v1_1.md), [digital/source distinctions](https://github.com/HIROMU1015/Partially-Randomized-Trotter/blob/af75af03c0b334c29b5a4311bd2b4578a999460c/docs/research/track_b_c1_4_5_c1_5_technical_preflight_20261021/SOURCE_CONNECTION_AUDIT.md).

The reported H4 records contain no quantum measurement shots and do not complete a coherent signal estimator. Deterministic halves, state preparation and readout costs, uniform strict native implementation costs, a certified sufficient trial budget, and rigorous binding to the saved DF exponential task remain unavailable. The empirical weight second moments are descriptive and cannot replace certified moments or range bounds. Different method-specific pseudorandom keys and unequal implementation/monitoring conditions further limit comparisons. Generalization to other molecules, evolution times, or Taylor degrees requires separate justification. [application limitations](https://github.com/HIROMU1015/Partially-Randomized-Trotter/blob/bb9059bacb697e1105e63c1b3826b0c34886ad15/docs/research/track_b_c1_5a_p5_worker_recovery_20261021/handoff.md).
