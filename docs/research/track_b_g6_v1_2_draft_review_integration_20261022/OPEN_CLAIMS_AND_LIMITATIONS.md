# Open claims and limits of the v1.2 draft

The draft is a document for user/GPT judgment, not formal scientific adoption. The following statuses describe evidence limits; they do not authorize a new experiment, proof, sampler, literature review or manuscript revision.

| Claim | Status | Existing reason / effect on wording |
|---|---|---|
| The complete construction is an independently original contribution | `NOT_ESTABLISHED` | Existing [PRIOR] / [PRIORCAND] identify known components and remaining correspondence questions. Neither priority nor non-reducibility is proved. The manuscript presents a bounded constructive contribution candidate |
| General Full improves total quantum resources for general inputs | `NOT_ESTABLISHED` | [G7], [G9], [E4] are fixed and conditional. Strong specializations, native prices, bounds and confidence policies can change the comparison |
| General Full beats Closed P5 by cheaper accepted quantum events at m=5 | `NOT_ESTABLISHED` | Their matched ideal accepted event law is equal. Different digital laws, acquisition, rejection, certification and classical processing remain |
| Ideal Full/canonical second-moment cancellation proves exact digital total-cost equality | `NOT_ESTABLISHED` | The supplied interpretation excludes coefficient/angle/native errors, ceil/range terms, conservative certificates, hard caps, terminal failure and classical costs |
| Small-x normalization formulas imply a uniform or molecular cost improvement | `NOT_ESTABLISHED` | Fixed p and fixed odd m≥3 only; O(x⁴) uniformity is not asserted. H4 χ was not evaluated |
| H4 establishes equal-accuracy total native superiority or inferiority of RA | `NOT_ESTABLISHED` | 1024 exploratory paths/method, no common certified N; only TAIL_ONLY_PLUS_SCALAR logical records. Native costs, deterministic halves, physical preparation/readout, complete coherent estimator, rigorous model binding and all-round RPE/QPE are missing |
| Degree-zero events are free identities or show zero aggregation effect | `UNSUPPORTED` | Child rotations remain and root coefficients contain higher-order returns |
| CPU observations identify optimized algorithmic performance | `NOT_ESTABLISHED` | Concurrency, selector/cache ownership, process start, monitoring and timing boundaries differ [C1COST] |
| H4 P5 has uniform canonical finite-bit / all-task certification | `NOT_ESTABLISHED` | Its source status remains `UNCERTIFIED_EXPLORATORY`. Event checks and Full preflight do not certify the missing canonical/physical/RNG conditions |
| Exact R1 dyadic inputs rigorously reproduce the physical DF exponential task | `NOT_ESTABLISHED` | Input exactness defines the adopted finite model. [R1BIND] remains `MODEL_BINDING_UNCLOSED` |
| P_U disproves all RA benefit | `UNSUPPORTED` | [PU] disproves a quantified upper-bound comparison. U is not the true RA normalizer |
| Submission GO, final title, final center claim and article type | `AWAITING_USER_GPT_DECISION` | Publication makes the draft reviewable; it does not make these decisions |

Existing F1/F2 specifications, F3 technical implementation evidence and the audited short-step ideal construction are retained with their original conditions; the above limits do not relabel those completed parts as wholly unstarted. No extra certificate-closure stage is made a prerequisite for this draft.

C1 remains `C1_PAUSED_AFTER_EXPLORATORY_RESOURCE_ASSESSMENT`. Existing Track B closures, grants, claims, ledgers, markers and STOP records retain their identities. After document publication and verification, this task enters mandatory STOP. The user/GPT may assess the draft's center claim, changes, article positioning and formal adoption; no subsequent work starts automatically.
